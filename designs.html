Process

How a project runs.

Four simple stages, from first message to final files.

  1. 01

    Brief

    You share the goal, the audience and any brand files you already have.

  2. 02

    Directions

    I explore a few different directions and show them side by side.

  3. 03

    Refine

    We pick one and tighten the type, colour and layout together.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    You receive print-ready and web-ready files in the formats you need.
