Design notes

How it holds together.

  1. One label grid serves the whole range: product name centred, nutrition facts right, storage notes left.
  2. A single background colour per spice makes jars easy to tell apart on a shelf.
  3. A small product illustration sits beside each name, so the contents read at a glance.
