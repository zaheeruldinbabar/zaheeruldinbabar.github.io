Design notes

How it holds together.

  1. One neon-blue palette on near-black ties the emblem, store screenshots and banners together.
  2. Every screenshot leads with a two-line benefit headline above the device, so the feature reads before the interface.
  3. Phone and desktop sets mirror each other, so one campaign covers both formats.
